# PrimeX — Draft Google Play Store listing

**App name:** PrimeX

**Short description:** Track personal goals, see your progress, and stay focused.

## Full description
PrimeX is a personal goal-tracking application that helps you organise your priorities and follow your progress. Sign in with Google, create goals, mark them complete, and view your progress through the Prime Score dashboard. Switch between English and isiZulu and access previously loaded goals when offline.

Current offline support is read-only: creating or completing goals requires internet access. Notifications are local app notifications rather than remote push notifications. The app is not yet publicly released on Google Play.
